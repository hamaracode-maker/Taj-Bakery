Our Story

Baked fresh, served with care, every single day.

Tucked into Gulzar‑e‑Hijri, Taj Bakery has become the neighbourhood's trusted stop for bread, pastries and celebration cakes. Everything on the counter is baked in small batches, the same day it's sold.

From a loaf for the morning table to a Black Forest cake for a birthday, we treat every order — big or small — as something worth doing properly.
